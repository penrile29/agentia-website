05 / FAQs

Before you start.

How does Oakbase work alongside our CRM?

Your CRM remains your record of clients and opportunities. Oakbase connects it with the work happening in emails, meetings and documents, building shared context. Your agent uses that context to keep your CRM and other connected systems up to date, within the permissions you set.

How does the firm’s brain connect information?

Oakbase starts with a legal ontology: clients, matters, people, documents and commitments. It uses this structure to connect information from authorised sources and preserve its origin. When a relationship is unclear, Oakbase asks the professional to confirm it within their workflow.

What does a prebuilt agent do for each role?

Agents come with administrative skills already prepared: proposing time entries, organising tasks, following up with clients or preparing invoices. Skills are matched to each role and use the firm’s shared memory, within its permissions and approval rules. You don’t have to design an agent from scratch.

Who decides what the agent can do?

Your firm decides which information each agent can use, which actions it can take and which require review. The agent can prepare proposals or carry out authorised actions in connected systems, according to those rules.

Do I need to use another application?

You can access Oakbase from Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot through its MCP connection. From that interface, your agent uses the firm’s context to find information and act in connected systems, within its configured permissions.
