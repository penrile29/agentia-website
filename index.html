Two modes of agentic work

Some agents talk. Others work quietly in the background.

The right modality depends on the job. Conversational agents create a dialogue surface. Headless agents execute operational work behind the scenes, with outcomes, exceptions and quality governed through the Agentia layer.

Conversational

When the job needs a dialogue.

Interfaces for customers or internal teams: support, sales qualification, knowledge access and guided service flows.

Primary surface Chat, voice, portal or embedded assistant
Headless

When the job needs execution.

Autonomous agents running in the background: documents, data enrichment, integrations, follow-up, routing and back-office workflows.

Primary surface Workflow triggers, MCP tools and exception queues
