    Security & confidentiality

    Your firm’s knowledge.
    Protected.

    Client relationships. Confidential matters. Years of expertise. The knowledge your practice is built on deserves clear safeguards.

    01 / Encryption

    Protected in transit.
    Encrypted at rest.

    Your firm’s data is encrypted as it moves between systems and while stored in Oakbase. Encryption makes it unreadable without the appropriate decryption keys.

    02 / EU residency

    Your data stays
    in the EU.

    Your firm’s data is stored and processed in the European Union. This gives your practice a clear location boundary for the information entrusted to Oakbase.

    03 / AI processing

    Zero Data Retention
    with AI providers.

    Our AI providers process requests and responses without retaining them after processing. Your firm’s data is not used to train AI models.

    A lasting memory for your firm. Zero Data Retention applies to AI providers. Firm Memory persists in Oakbase, under your firm’s control.
