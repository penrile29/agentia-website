04 / EXAMPLE

Time Capture operation

Time capture in a mid-size law firm

From work signals to approved time, using authorised matters, activity, permissions and approval rules.

  1. 01 Systems Get Customers & Matters

    Use that professional’s permissions to retrieve their active matters and authorised client context from the CRM and matter system.

  2. 02 Evidence Analyse documents

    Detect drafting, review and matter activity across approved documents.

  3. 03 Activity Analyse calendars

    Match meetings and events to matters and likely billable work.

  4. 04 Activity Analyse email

    Identify matter-related work and follow-ups within approved access.

  5. 05 Proposal Calculate billable time

    Reconcile activity, remove duplicates and calculate draft billable entries by matter.

  6. 06 Control Human review

    Send proposed entries, evidence and exceptions to that professional for confirmation.

05 / HOW WE WORK

From first workflow to managed operations

One useful operation in four weeks.

We start with one high-value operational job, prove it in production, then operate and expand the agentic system with your team.

Operating journey Weeks 01–04 → ongoing
  1. 01 Week 01 Discover

    Map the job, establish the baseline and define a measurable outcome.

  2. 02 Week 02 Design

    Define the target flow, permissions, human decisions and exception paths.

  3. 03 Weeks 03–04 Deploy

    Connect the systems, configure the agents and test on real work.

  4. 04 Week 04 Prove

    Run in production, measure quality and capacity, and agree the next operation.

  5. 05 Monthly Operate

    Monitor quality, resolve exceptions, improve live agents and deploy the next operations.
