Oakbase/Law firms/Time Capture

Managed Time Capture operation

Recover billable work without chasing timesheets.

Oakbase identifies likely missing time, prepares entries with supporting work signals and sends them to the lawyer for approval before anything is recorded.

One professional. One operation.

Each lawyer’s operation uses only their authorised matters, activity, permissions and approval rules.

Professional judgement

The lawyer stays in control.

  • Confirms the matter and narrative
  • Corrects the proposed duration
  • Rejects unsuitable entries
  • Approves the final record

Operational execution

Oakbase prepares the work.

  • Collects permitted activity signals
  • Matches work to authorised matters
  • Proposes structured entries
  • Writes back only after approval

02 / OPERATION

From work signals to approved time

Time capture in a law firm.

The operation works alongside specialist legal tools and the firm’s matter, document, calendar, email and time-recording systems. Access remains limited to what that professional is authorised to use.

Illustrative operation — not customer results

This sequence shows how the operating model works. It contains no claimed customer performance, hours recovered or time saved.

  1. 01 / SYSTEMS

    Get customers and matters

    Retrieve only the active matters and client context authorised for that lawyer.

  2. 02 / EVIDENCE

    Analyse documents

    Detect drafting, review and matter activity across approved document systems.

  3. 03 / ACTIVITY

    Analyse calendars

    Match meetings and events to matters and identify likely billable work.

  4. 04 / ACTIVITY

    Analyse email

    Identify matter-related work and follow-through within approved access.

  5. 05 / PROPOSAL

    Calculate billable time

    Reconcile the evidence, remove duplicates and prepare draft entries by matter.

  6. 06 / CONTROL

    Human review

    Send proposed entries and exceptions to the lawyer, then record only what they approve.
