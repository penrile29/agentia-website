# Oakbase — messaging pivot

Direction supplied by the founder, 18 September 2026, with security positioning added on 21 September. Applied to the English V2 preview on GitHub Pages; this document records the positioning, not an implementation audit.

## Core proposition

Oakbase convierte el conocimiento y las relaciones que los profesionales construyen cada día en un activo duradero de la firma: una memoria consultable y accionable, utilizada por agentes preconstruidos para ejecutar Operaciones Gestionadas, con visibilidad y control sobre su uso.

**English:** Oakbase turns the knowledge built through everyday professional work into a lasting firm asset. Prebuilt agents use that memory to run Managed Operations, while the firm controls access and can see how its information is used.

## Message hierarchy

| Promise | Business value | Homepage expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firm Memory | Expertise, relationships and decisions remain usable beyond individual conversations and engagements. People can find answers; agents can act with context. | “Your firm’s knowledge. A lasting asset.” / “A brain for your firm.” |
| Managed Operations | Prebuilt agents reduce administration and keep client, matter, time and billing records aligned with the work. Oakbase configures, runs and improves the operation. | “Less admin. More reliable records.” |
| Visibility and control | The firm can understand information relationships, inspect use and actions, and restrict what people and agents can access or do. | “See the work. Control the access.” |
| Security and confidentiality | Protect the client relationships, confidential matters and accumulated expertise on which a law practice depends. | “Your firm’s knowledge. Protected.” |

## How the story develops

1. The hero leads with the firm asset and names expertise, relationships, prebuilt agents and control.
2. The brain shows the progression from knowledge captured in work to persistent memory, agents and complete operations. The memory detail explains retention, queries, relationships and reuse.
3. Time Capture demonstrates the promise through an open timeline: Firm Memory supplies client/matter context and work activity; the agent proposes time; the professional reviews it; approved entries are logged. The illustrative week shows 38.5 hours proposed, 34.0 logged and 4.5 pending review. These are demonstration figures, not customer results or a productivity claim.
4. The security section explains encryption in transit/at rest, EU data residency and Zero Data Retention with AI providers in visible prose. A separate sentence distinguishes AI-provider non-retention from persistent Firm Memory. The control row and its detail then explain connections, visibility, access and actions.
5. The final invitation is to put the firm’s knowledge to work through one Managed Operation. The previous four-week delivery headline no longer leads the closing message.

## Language choices

- Translate “fondo de comercio” as **a lasting firm asset**, supported by expertise and relationship knowledge. Avoid the accounting term “goodwill” and any claim of a balance-sheet valuation.
- Use **Firm Memory** for the product concept and the brain for the visual metaphor. Explain that the knowledge is persistent, searchable and usable by people and agents.
- Name **prebuilt agents** explicitly. Oakbase adapts and operates them around the firm’s work; the customer is not being asked to build an agent platform.
- Express “system-of-record health” through concrete outcomes: current client records, coherent matter history, reviewed time and invoice-ready records.
- Keep professional judgement, agreed approvals and access boundaries explicit. Visibility and access control are separate benefits.
- Explain encryption as making data unreadable without the appropriate keys, residency as the location where firm data is stored and processed, and Zero Data Retention as AI providers not retaining requests/responses after processing. No training is a separate promise. Do not describe Oakbase’s persistent Firm Memory as transient or imply that encryption is end-to-end or customer-keyed.
- Avoid leading with generic AI productivity, a list of technologies or implementation disclaimers. Keep the local-preview status and illustrative examples visible, with release alignment notes in the preview detail and internal claims review.

## Scope

The user’s description is the authority for this positioning, including the explicit encryption, EU residency and AI Zero Data Retention commitments supplied on 21 September. No customer metrics, certifications, specific encryption algorithms, customer-managed keys or legal-privilege guarantees were added. The GitHub Pages preview carries this messaging; existing policy pages and oakbase.ai are unchanged. Historical policy observations in V2-CLAIMS-REVIEW.md remain relevant to production policy alignment, not as a competing product positioning.
