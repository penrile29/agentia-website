03 / MODEL

Define before deploying

One operating model, grounded in the firm.

Oakbase works alongside specialist advice tools and the business systems already used by the firm. The appropriate professional retains final judgement and approval.

  1. 01Map

    Understand the current process, systems, controls and points of operational friction.

  2. 02Measure

    Set a baseline and define an output that can be checked against business results.

  3. 03Control

    Specify permitted data, tool access, execution policies and human approvals.

  4. 04Operate

    Deploy, run and improve the operation when the process and controls are ready.
